Stock Hush Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026 Last updated: October 19, 2026

Stock Hush ("the app") is an iOS app made by Raghav Verma, a sole proprietor based in Toronto, Ontario, Canada ("I", "me"). This policy explains what information the app uses and what happens to it. The short version: I don't have accounts, I don't run analytics or advertising, and your holdings stay on your phone.

What stays on your device

The following is stored only on your iPhone and is never sent to me or to anyone else:

Your holdings: tickers, number of shares and the price you paid.
Your alert threshold and your app settings.
Your Screen Time selection: which apps you chose to shield. Apple's Screen Time framework gives the app only anonymous tokens for these apps. The app can't see their names, and the selection is never transmitted.
Cached prices the app has downloaded, used to show your status and the shield message.

Some of this is kept in a storage area shared between the app and its shield extensions, so the shield screen can show your status. That storage is local to your device.

What is sent off your device

To check prices, the app sends ticker symbols only (for example "NVDA") to a price service I operate. It also sends the text you type when you search for a ticker. The app does not send your share counts, the prices you paid, your thresholds, or any information that identifies you.

My price service uses a third-party market-data provider (Twelve Data) to look up quotes. Only ticker symbols and search text reach that provider. Your device and your IP address do not.

Like any internet service, my server provider (Cloudflare) may process technical data such as your IP address and request details as part of delivering the service and protecting it from abuse. I don't use this data to identify or profile you, and I don't link it to any account, because there are no accounts.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. I never see your payment details, your name or your Apple Account. The app checks your purchase status on your device using Apple's StoreKit framework. Apple's own privacy policy governs how Apple handles that information.

What I don't do
I don't collect your name, email address, phone number or location.
I don't use analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs.
I don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.
I don't sell or share personal information.
Screen Time permission

The app asks for Screen Time access so it can shield the investing apps you choose. This permission is managed by iOS. You can turn it off at any time in iOS Settings, and shielding will stop working until you turn it back on.

Your choices
Delete a holding in the app at any time to remove it from your device.
Delete the app to remove all data it stores on your device.
Manage or cancel a subscription in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
Children

Stock Hush is not directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If I change this policy, I'll update the date above. If a change is material, I'll say so in the app's release notes.
