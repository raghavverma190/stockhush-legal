**Common questions**

**How do I cancel my subscription?** Open iOS Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Stock Hush. You can also tap Manage in App Store inside the app (Settings → Go Quiet).

**I bought on another device, or reinstalled.** Open the Go Quiet screen and tap Restore.

**Why don't prices update instantly?** Prices refresh about every 70 minutes, and only when the app is opened or iOS allows a background refresh. This keeps Stock Hush quiet.

**Does Stock Hush see my brokerage account?** No. It never connects to your brokerage. You enter your holdings yourself, and they stay on your phone.
